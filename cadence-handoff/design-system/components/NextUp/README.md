The single card that names the one thing to do now.

**You provide:** one beat (habit or task). The card shows its title, its cue or due time, and two actions.

- Exactly one item, never a list or carousel. Selection order: overdue tasks by priority, then the habit whose time-of-day group is current, then the next timed task.
- **Mark done** completes it and the card swaps to the next beat. **Later** moves it to the end of today's order without any penalty or wording about skipping.
- For a habit, the meta line is its cue and minimum; for a task, due time and duration.
- When nothing is left: the card stays, title "That's today", line "8 of 8 beats done. Rest well.", no buttons.
- **Motion:** on completion the old content fades and lifts 8px (`duration-quick`, `ease-in`), the new content rises 12px into place (`duration-slow`, `ease-out`). The card itself never moves, so the eye has a fixed anchor.
- It is the only `glass-raised` card in the scroll, and holds the screen's only primary button.
