Yours, on your phone

No account. No network. No audience.

Cadence never asks who you are and never goes online. Everything you write stays in a database on the device.

Stored locally

Habits, logs, tasks and notes live in a SQLite database on your phone. Settings sit beside them.

Works offline, always

There is no sync, no sign-in and no server. Aeroplane mode changes nothing.

Notes are plain Markdown

Headings, lists, to-dos, quotes and code. Type / for the menu, or use the shortcuts you already know. Export any note as a .md file.

One reminder a day

A single daily nudge at the time you choose. Nothing else ever notifies you.

Take it with you

Export your data whenever you like. It is yours, in a format you can read.

Built to be read

Text holds at least 4.5:1 contrast in every palette. High contrast themes reach 7:1, and every state has a shape as well as a colour.
